# Third-party notices

## Qt

GetRight Port links dynamically to Qt Core, Gui, Widgets, and Network. Deployment can also include
Qt DBus and supporting libraries required by those frameworks. Distributed
macOS bundles include Qt frameworks and the Cocoa, offscreen, macOS style, and native
TLS plugins. Qt is copyright The Qt Company Ltd. and other contributors.

Qt is available under the GNU LGPL version 3 and other licenses. See
[Qt licensing](https://doc.qt.io/qt-6/licensing.html) and the
[Qt source archive](https://download.qt.io/official_releases/qt/).
The release notes identify the bundled Qt version. Build instructions are in README.md;
the libraries remain dynamically linked so they can be replaced and the app rebuilt.
